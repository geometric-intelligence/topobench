If the summary and LICENSE differ, LICENSE contains the governing terms.

WHO CAN USE TOPOBENCH FOR FREE?

Eligible users may use TopoBench for noncommercial research, teaching,
learning and reproducing results, without registration or separate approval.
This includes universities, nonprofit research organisations and public
research bodies acting in that capacity, and individuals doing independent
research outside work for a company. See clauses 1 and 2 for the full scope.

For example, a university researcher may run benchmarks, train models and
publish a paper. Receiving a salary, scholarship or ordinary research grant
does not by itself make that research commercial.

WHEN IS A COMMERCIAL AGREEMENT REQUIRED?

Apart from the limited infrastructure permission below, company use is
excluded from this research licence. This includes internal research,
benchmarks, trials, proofs of concept, employee training and evaluation,
even if there are no sales or the results will be published. Commercial
activities of universities and nonprofits are also excluded.

An employee may conduct eligible independent personal research, but work
within company duties, on company time, using employer-provided resources
or on its behalf is excluded. A personal research licence cannot authorise
company use. Excluded use requires a separate written commercial agreement
with the relevant rights holders or their authorised representatives before
it begins. Contact topological.intelligence@gmail.com for enquiries.

WHAT ABOUT COMPANY-FUNDED RESEARCH?

An independent grant can qualify if researchers retain control over their
research and freedom to publish. For example, a company can fund a
university's independent research and receive the same public results as
everyone else. Funding acknowledgements and expenditure reports are fine.

Company-directed or commissioned work, private deliverables, company-specific
evaluations and exclusive sponsor rights to results are excluded. For example,
benchmarking products for a company's private purchasing decision requires
a commercial agreement. Funding does not let the sponsor use TopoBench.

CAN I SHARE OR MODIFY THE CODE?

Yes, for eligible research, subject to clause 3. When distributing covered
code or modifications, include the complete unchanged licence, required
notices and citation instructions, and identify changes and their dates.
Provide complete corresponding source, including modifications and necessary
build or installation scripts, with the copy or at a working download link
without additional charge for as long as you distribute that version.

Your covered modifications must be offered under the same TopoBench Research
Licence 1.0, and you must have authority to grant those rights. Do not add
terms, fees or technical restrictions that prevent recipients from exercising
the permissions. Each recipient must independently qualify for their use.
An upstream link that omits your modifications is not sufficient.

This sharing rule covers TopoBench code and derivative modifications.
It does not automatically apply to independent programs, papers, datasets
or model weights simply because they were used with or produced by TopoBench.
Embedded TopoBench code and separately licensed material retain their terms.
See NOTICE and the accompanying third-party notices where applicable.

Published or publicly distributed outputs describing work performed using
TopoBench must acknowledge TopoBench, identify the version or commit used,
and cite the principal publication:

TopoBench: A Framework for Benchmarking Topological Deep Learning
https://openreview.net/forum?id=07sTzyEVtY

The README contains a copyable BibTeX entry. The citation format required by
your journal, conference or other venue is acceptable. This is a licence
condition, not just a request.

DO I HAVE TO PUBLISH OR CONTRIBUTE MY WORK?

Publication and contributions are encouraged, but the research licence does
not require you to publish private research results or modifications, or
submit a pull request. If you distribute covered code or modifications,
the sharing conditions above apply. Publishing results alone does not
require releasing private code under the current licence.

Eligible researchers may publish through commercial journals or publishers.
For example, paying a journal's publication fee does not disqualify eligible
research. This permission does not let a publisher or another company run
TopoBench for its own research or business activities.

CAN I USE GITHUB, PACKAGE HOSTING OR CLOUD COMPUTE?

Yes. Clause 5 allows ordinary hosting, package distribution, storage,
networking and rented compute for eligible research or sharing under clause 3.
For example, an eligible researcher may rent a cloud GPU to run experiments,
or use a hosting service to distribute a modified TopoBench version with its
source under the same licence. Ordinary infrastructure fees are permitted.

Providers may copy, transmit and execute TopoBench only as necessary to
supply that infrastructure on the researcher's instructions. They receive
no permission for their own research, benchmarking, model training, product
development or TopoBench-based services. Those uses require a separate
commercial agreement.

HOW DO CONTRIBUTIONS TO TOPOBENCH WORK?

Contributions are welcome. CONTRIBUTING.md explains how to submit them and
accept CLA.md, the TopoBench Contributor Agreement. Contributors retain
ownership. The agreement provides research permissions and an additional
royalty-free commercial licensing grant to the recipients identified in the
agreement. Read the version linked in the PR template and personally select its
acceptance checkbox. No separate email or form is required. The PR records the
acceptance; the automated check checks for the acknowledgement, not ownership
or authority. Each new PR requires its own acceptance.
